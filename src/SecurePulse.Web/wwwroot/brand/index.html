Typography / Google Sans Flex
Clarity at
every scale.

Google Sans Flex throughout the interface and the Hasinix wordmark. The logo uses weight 650 with carefully spaced lowercase lettering. Downloadable SVGs contain the actual font outlines, so they render consistently without installed fonts.

Use / Give it room

Give the falcon room.

Leave clear space of at least half the shield's width around the logo. Use the mark alone below 96px. Keep the orange and navy colors, and choose the logo made for its background. Do not stretch or rotate the artwork.
